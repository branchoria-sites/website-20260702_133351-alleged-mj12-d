## Introduction

One of the most frequently cited arguments in favour of the alleged MJ-12 [Truman authorisation]({{'truman-memo/' | relative_url }}) memo is that its date was not invented from thin air. On 24 September 1947, President Harry S. Truman really was scheduled to meet Secretary of Defense [James Forrestal]({{'forrestal/' | relative_url }}) and scientist Vannevar Bush at the White House. That genuine appointment gives the alleged memo an immediate appearance of authenticity. However, the historical question is not whether the meeting occurred, but whether the existence of the meeting provides evidence that Truman signed the alleged directive creating"Operation Majestic Twelve". The available documentary record shows only that the meeting was scheduled. It does not document the meeting's subject, nor does it establish that a presidential order resembling the alleged MJ-12 memorandum was issued. ## What the appointment calendar actually shows

The Harry S. Truman [Presidential Library]({{'library-searches/' | relative_url }}) preserves the President's appointment records, and these show that an 11:30 a.m. meeting involving Harry Truman, James Forrestal and Dr [Vannevar Bush]({{'bush/' | relative_url }}) was scheduled for 24 September 1947. This is a genuine historical record and is not disputed.

The timing is also historically plausible in a broader sense. The [National Security]({{'1947-context/' | relative_url }}) Act had only recently taken effect, James Forrestal had just become the first Secretary of Defense, and Vannevar Bush remained one of the United States' most influential scientific advisers. A White House discussion involving these figures during a period of major defence reorganisation would have been entirely routine.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Security_Act_of_1947" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">National Security Act of 1947</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">National Security Act of 1947</span></span></span>

What the calendar does **not** provide is equally important. Appointment books generally record who met, when they met and sometimes who requested the meeting. They rarely constitute evidence of what was discussed, whether formal decisions were taken or whether any classified directives were signed. The calendar therefore establishes only the existence of an opportunity for discussion, not the substance of any presidential action.

## Why a real date can help a forged document

From the perspective of document authentication, using a genuine meeting date is a recognised way of making a fabricated document appear convincing.

A convincing forgery often incorporates verifiable historical details because those details survive independent checking. If an investigator confirms that Truman, Forrestal and Bush were indeed together on the stated day, the document immediately acquires an impression of factual grounding, even though the crucial claim—the existence of an MJ-12 authorisation—remains unproven.

The alleged Truman memorandum appears to exploit precisely this effect. Rather than inventing an imaginary conference, it places its dramatic claims within a documented presidential appointment. The authentic calendar entry therefore functions as contextual support for the narrative without independently supporting the memorandum itself.

This is one reason historians distinguish between:

* **External consistency**, where names, dates and locations match genuine historical records.
* **Document [authenticity]({{'authenticity/' | relative_url }})**, which requires evidence that the document itself originated from the claimed author and governmental record system.

## Why the meeting alone does not authenticate the memo

The evidential gap between a scheduled meeting and a presidential directive is substantial.

A genuine presidential authorisation of a new ultra-secret organisation would normally be expected to leave at least some administrative footprint beyond an appointment diary. Depending on the security procedures involved, researchers might expect to find:<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--decision" markdown="1">

* draft memoranda or routing slips;
* filing references within White House records;
* archival copies bearing established document-control features;
* related correspondence among officials responsible for implementing the decision;
* later references acknowledging the directive's existence.</div>

None of these naturally follows from the appointment calendar itself. The calendar merely confirms that three senior officials were expected to be in the same room.

This distinction explains why archivists treat appointment books as contextual evidence rather than proof of policy decisions.<img src="{{"/assets/images/alleged_mj12_documen_5d9d42_truman_authorisation_aafe47_truman_meeting_camou_67f598-Illustration-2-dark.svg" | relative_url }}" alt="Meeting Date illustration 2" data-theme-src-dark="{{"/assets/images/alleged_mj12_documen_5d9d42_truman_authorisation_aafe47_truman_meeting_camou_67f598-Illustration-2-dark.svg" | relative_url }}" data-theme-src-light="{{"/assets/images/alleged_mj12_documen_5d9d42_truman_authorisation_aafe47_truman_meeting_camou_67f598-Illustration-2-light.svg" | relative_url }}" loading="lazy" decoding="async" fetchpriority="low">
## How researchers interpret the coincidence

Supporters of MJ-12 often argue that the meeting's existence strengthens the memo because the date was not widely known when the alleged documents surfaced in the 1980s. From this perspective, selecting an authentic appointment would have required access to archival material unavailable to casual researchers.

Critics respond that even if the meeting date was obscure, discovering a genuine appointment does not establish the authenticity of a separate document referring to that date. A forger with archival access—or someone consulting genuine presidential records—could deliberately anchor a fabricated memorandum to a real historical event.

Document examiners therefore separate two questions:

## What evidence would be needed beyond the meeting

If historians were assessing the alleged Truman memorandum without preconceived views about MJ-12, they would seek corroborating records rather than relying on the calendar entry alone.

Examples of stronger supporting evidence would include:

* an authenticated White House file copy with documented archival [provenance]({{'provenance/' | relative_url }});<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* contemporaneous correspondence referring to the directive;
* document registration numbers matching established White House filing practices;
* implementation records created by agencies instructed to carry out the order;
* consistent references appearing independently across multiple official archives.</div>

The appointment calendar supplies none of these elements. It provides historical context but not documentary corroboration.

This is why professional document analysis treats the meeting as a starting point for investigation rather than the conclusion of one.<img src="{{"/assets/images/alleged_mj12_documen_5d9d42_truman_authorisation_aafe47_truman_meeting_camou_67f598-Illustration-3-dark.svg" | relative_url }}" alt="Meeting Date illustration 3" data-theme-src-dark="{{"/assets/images/alleged_mj12_documen_5d9d42_truman_authorisation_aafe47_truman_meeting_camou_67f598-Illustration-3-dark.svg" | relative_url }}" data-theme-src-light="{{"/assets/images/alleged_mj12_documen_5d9d42_truman_authorisation_aafe47_truman_meeting_camou_67f598-Illustration-3-light.svg" | relative_url }}" loading="lazy" decoding="async" fetchpriority="low">
## Why the meeting remains important in the MJ-12 debate

The 24 September 1947 meeting remains significant because it illustrates how genuine historical records can increase the [plausibility]({{'plausibility/' | relative_url }}) of disputed documents without proving them authentic.

For readers encountering the alleged memo for the first time, the matching date can appear persuasive: Truman really did meet Forrestal and Bush. Yet historians distinguish between a verified historical setting and a verified presidential action. The former is established by surviving White House records; the latter requires independent evidence that has not been found.

As a result, the real meeting is best understood as contextual camouflage rather than confirmation.     if (reference) params.push('customid=' + encodeURIComponent(reference).slice(0, 256));
    return url + joiner + params.join('&');
  }
  function domainLabel(marketId) {
    var cfg = markets[marketId] || markets[defaultMarket] || {};
    return cfg.domain_label ||'eBay';
  }
  function flagClass(marketId) {
    return'fr-ebay-market-flag fr-ebay-market-flag--' + String(marketId ||'').toLowerCase().replace(/_/g,'-').replace(/[^a-z0-9-]+/g,'');
  }
  function closeMenu(section) {
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var picker = section.querySelector('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
    if (menu) menu.hidden = true;
    if (trigger) trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded','false');
    if (picker) picker.classList.remove('fr-ebay-market-picker--open');
  }
  function availableMarkets(section) {
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      return Array.prototype.slice.call(select.options)
        .map(function (option) { return option.value; })
        .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
    }
    return Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]'))
      .map(function (button) { return button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'); })
      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') ||'';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') ||'';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ?'true' :'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ?'true' :'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') ==='1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init','1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey:'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') ==='true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ?'false' :'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
