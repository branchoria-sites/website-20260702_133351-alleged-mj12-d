## Introduction

The significance of the Doty–Moore–Pratt relationship lies less in any single document than in the way information circulated before the alleged MJ-12 papers emerged publicly. Rather than a straightforward chain running from a government archive to researchers, the available evidence points to a small network in which alleged insider claims, researchers' own historical theories, and draft narratives repeatedly passed between three central figures: Air Force Office of Special Investigations ([AFOSI]({{'afosi/' | relative_url }})) sergeant Richard Doty, UFO researcher William L. Moore, and journalist Robert Pratt. This circulation does not prove that the later MJ-12 documents were fabricated by any particular individual. It does, however, complicate claims that they arrived as an entirely independent leak, because many of their key themes appear to have been discussed privately years before the documents became public.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.academia.edu/24214339/The_Secret_Pratt_Tapes_and_the_Origins_of_MJ_12_Brad_Sparks_and_Barry_Greenwood_" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: academia.edu">[Academia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">academia.edu</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The Secret Pratt Tapes and the Origins of MJ-12 (Brad...&quot;The Secret Pratt Tapes and the Origins of MJ-12&quot; is a paper by US UFO r...</span></span></span><img src="{{"/assets/images/alleged_mj12_documen_5d9d42_pratt_tapes_a5fb50_doty_moore_pratt_cha_c6256e-Illustration-1-dark.svg" | relative_url }}" alt="Rumor Channel illustration 1" data-theme-src-dark="{{"/assets/images/alleged_mj12_documen_5d9d42_pratt_tapes_a5fb50_doty_moore_pratt_cha_c6256e-Illustration-1-dark.svg" | relative_url }}" data-theme-src-light="{{"/assets/images/alleged_mj12_documen_5d9d42_pratt_tapes_a5fb50_doty_moore_pratt_cha_c6256e-Illustration-1-light.svg" | relative_url }}" loading="eager" decoding="sync" fetchpriority="high">
## Who Moore, Pratt and Doty were

Each participant occupied a different position within the emerging UFO controversy.

**William L. Moore** was one of the leading civilian investigators of the Roswell incident during the late 1970s and early 1980s. His research focused on military personnel, government records and possible evidence for official secrecy. Because of his reputation, new claims passed to Moore often acquired credibility within UFO [research circles]({{'research-circle/' | relative_url }}) before wider scrutiny.

**Robert Pratt** was an experienced journalist who maintained close contact with UFO researchers while continuing to approach stories as a reporter. According to later research, Pratt secretly recorded numerous conversations with Moore during 1982. Those recordings became valuable because they captured discussions before the MJ-12 documents entered public debate, preserving an earlier stage in the evolution of the narrative.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.academia.edu/24214339/The_Secret_Pratt_Tapes_and_the_Origins_of_MJ_12_Brad_Sparks_and_Barry_Greenwood_" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: academia.edu">[Academia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">academia.edu</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The Secret Pratt Tapes and the Origins of MJ-12 (Brad...&quot;The Secret Pratt Tapes and the Origins of MJ-12&quot; is a paper by US UFO r...</span></span></span>

## How information moved through the network

The most important feature of this three-person network is that information rarely travelled in only one direction.

According to the Pratt tapes analysed by Brad Sparks and Barry Greenwood, Moore did not simply receive alleged classified information from Doty. Instead, Moore discussed his own historical research, theories about presidential transitions, Roswell chronology and possible participants in a secret government programme. Doty, in turn, supplied alleged insider confirmations, [code names]({{'code-names/' | relative_url }}) and purported classified background. Pratt documented these exchanges through recordings and notes.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.academia.edu/24214339/The_Secret_Pratt_Tapes_and_the_Origins_of_MJ_12_Brad_Sparks_and_Barry_Greenwood_" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: academia.edu">[Academia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">academia.edu</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The Secret Pratt Tapes and the Origins of MJ-12 (Brad...&quot;The Secret Pratt Tapes and the Origins of MJ-12&quot; is a paper by US UFO r...</span></span></span>

This created what researchers often describe as a [feedback loop]({{'feedback-loop/' | relative_url }}) rather than a leak.

A simplified model looks like this:<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* Moore developed historical hypotheses through archival research.
* Doty responded with alleged insider material that appeared to validate or expand those ideas.
* Moore treated some of those responses as corroboration.
* Pratt recorded conversations in which these evolving concepts were discussed privately.
* Similar concepts later appeared in alleged classified MJ-12 documents.</div>

The important point is not that every idea originated with Moore or Doty, but that the same themes circulated repeatedly within a very small circle before emerging in documentary form.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.academia.edu/24214339/The_Secret_Pratt_Tapes_and_the_Origins_of_MJ_12_Brad_Sparks_and_Barry_Greenwood_" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: academia.edu">[Academia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">academia.edu</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The Secret Pratt Tapes and the Origins of MJ-12 (Brad...&quot;The Secret Pratt Tapes and the Origins of MJ-12&quot; is a paper by US UFO r...</span></span></span><img src="{{"/assets/images/alleged_mj12_documen_5d9d42_pratt_tapes_a5fb50_doty_moore_pratt_cha_c6256e-Illustration-2-dark.svg" | relative_url }}" alt="Rumor Channel illustration 2" data-theme-src-dark="{{"/assets/images/alleged_mj12_documen_5d9d42_pratt_tapes_a5fb50_doty_moore_pratt_cha_c6256e-Illustration-2-dark.svg" | relative_url }}" data-theme-src-light="{{"/assets/images/alleged_mj12_documen_5d9d42_pratt_tapes_a5fb50_doty_moore_pratt_cha_c6256e-Illustration-2-light.svg" | relative_url }}" loading="lazy" decoding="async" fetchpriority="low">
## Why the small circle matters for provenance

For historians, [provenance]({{'provenance/' | relative_url }}) means reconstructing where a document came from and how its contents developed before publication.

If a supposedly secret government document contains information unavailable to its recipients, that strengthens the argument that it derives from an independent archival source.

The Doty–Moore–Pratt network presents the opposite problem.

Research on the Pratt tapes argues that several distinctive elements later associated with MJ-12—including discussion of"[Aquarius]({{'aquarius/' | relative_url }})", a presidential transition briefing in late 1952, and the concept of a small elite control group—were already being exchanged privately before the alleged MJ-12 papers surfaced. That chronology means those ideas cannot automatically be treated as evidence of an authentic classified source simply because they later appeared in a document.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.academia.edu/24214339/The_Secret_Pratt_Tapes_and_the_Origins_of_MJ_12_Brad_Sparks_and_Barry_Greenwood_" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: academia.edu">[Academia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">academia.edu</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The Secret Pratt Tapes and the Origins of MJ-12 (Brad...&quot;The Secret Pratt Tapes and the Origins of MJ-12&quot; is a paper by US UFO r...</span></span></span>

The issue is therefore not merely whether Doty supplied false information. It is that researchers' own developing theories and alleged insider confirmations may have become intertwined, making it difficult to distinguish original archival evidence from concepts that evolved within the research community itself.<div class="youtube-embed-container youtube-embed-fallback"><div class="youtube-embed-card"><div class="youtube-embed-frame"><iframe src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/kOT39FXstho" title="&#x27;Mislead the Public&#x27; Ep. 3 Official Clip | UFO | SHOWTIME Documentary Series" frameborder="0" allow="accelerometer; autoplay; clipboard-write; encrypted-media; gyroscope; picture-in-picture; web-share" allowfullscreen></iframe></div><div class="youtube-embed-footer"><p class="youtube-embed-title"><a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kOT39FXstho" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">&#x27;Mislead the Public&#x27; Ep. 3 Official Clip | UFO | SHOWTIME Documentary Series</a></p><p class="youtube-embed-meta">Channel: SHOWTIME &middot; Views: 361.6K &middot; Uploaded: August 2021 &middot; Length: 1 minute 29 seconds</p><p class="youtube-embed-actions"><a class="youtube-embed-watch-link" href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kOT39FXstho" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" title="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kOT39FXstho">Open on YouTube</a></p></div></div></div>

## A feedback loop rather than independent confirmation

One of the strongest criticisms of [the alleged MJ-12 documents]({{'the-alleged-mj-12-documents/' | relative_url }}) is that they often appear to confirm information already known within this network.

Sparks and Greenwood argue that Moore and fellow researcher Stanton Friedman had already identified many individuals they believed would have participated in any highly classified Roswell investigation. Moore discussed this work with Doty before the alleged MJ-12 briefing papers appeared. Later, many of the same names appeared in the documents themselves. Rather than treating this as evidence against authenticity, some supporters initially regarded the overlap as confirmation that the documents reflected genuine history. Critics instead argued that such overlap is exactly what would be expected if a forger had access to Moore's research.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.academia.edu/24214339/The_Secret_Pratt_Tapes_and_the_Origins_of_MJ_12_Brad_Sparks_and_Barry_Greenwood_" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: academia.edu">[Academia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">academia.edu</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The Secret Pratt Tapes and the Origins of MJ-12 (Brad...&quot;The Secret Pratt Tapes and the Origins of MJ-12&quot; is a paper by US UFO r...</span></span></span>

Even Stanton Friedman acknowledged a logical possibility that, if the papers were fraudulent, they would likely have been created by someone with insider access to both government procedures and contemporary UFO research. That concession illustrates the central provenance problem: close contact between researchers and alleged insiders makes later documentary"confirmation" harder to interpret.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.academia.edu/24214339/The_Secret_Pratt_Tapes_and_the_Origins_of_MJ_12_Brad_Sparks_and_Barry_Greenwood_" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: academia.edu">[Academia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">academia.edu</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The Secret Pratt Tapes and the Origins of MJ-12 (Brad...&quot;The Secret Pratt Tapes and the Origins of MJ-12&quot; is a paper by US UFO r...</span></span></span><img src="{{"/assets/images/alleged_mj12_documen_5d9d42_pratt_tapes_a5fb50_doty_moore_pratt_cha_c6256e-Illustration-3-dark.svg" | relative_url }}" alt="Rumor Channel illustration 3" data-theme-src-dark="{{"/assets/images/alleged_mj12_documen_5d9d42_pratt_tapes_a5fb50_doty_moore_pratt_cha_c6256e-Illustration-3-dark.svg" | relative_url }}" data-theme-src-light="{{"/assets/images/alleged_mj12_documen_5d9d42_pratt_tapes_a5fb50_doty_moore_pratt_cha_c6256e-Illustration-3-light.svg" | relative_url }}" loading="lazy" decoding="async" fetchpriority="low">
## Why historians remain cautious

The existence of the Doty–Moore–Pratt communication channel does not establish that any one member created the alleged MJ-12 documents. Nor does it conclusively demonstrate an organised government disinformation programme directed specifically at the MJ-12 papers.

Instead, the network changes the historical question.

Rather than asking whether the documents suddenly appeared from an unknown classified archive, historians increasingly ask how ideas moved through a small group before publication.
