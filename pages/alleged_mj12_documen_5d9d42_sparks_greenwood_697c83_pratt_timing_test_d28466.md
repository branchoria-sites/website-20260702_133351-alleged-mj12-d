## Introduction

The Robert Pratt tapes occupy a distinctive place in debates over [the alleged MJ-12 documents]({{'the-alleged-mj-12-documents/' | relative_url }}) because they function as a chronology test rather than a document-authentication test. Brad Sparks and Barry Greenwood argue that the recordings show core MJ-12 concepts being discussed in private during 1982—around two years before the anonymous film containing the alleged MJ-12 papers reached Jaime Shandera in December 1984. If those ideas were already circulating among a small network of UFO researchers and intelligence contacts, then the later appearance of the documents becomes less persuasive as independent corroboration and more consistent with the possibility that the documents reflected ideas already developing within that circle.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.academia.edu/24214339/The_Secret_Pratt_Tapes_and_the_Origins_of_MJ_12_Brad_Sparks_and_Barry_Greenwood_" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: academia.edu">[Academia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">academia.edu</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The Secret Pratt Tapes and the Origins of MJ-12 (Brad...&quot;The Secret Pratt Tapes and the Origins of MJ-12&quot; is a paper by US UFO r...</span></span></span><img src="{{"/assets/images/alleged_mj12_documen_5d9d42_sparks_greenwood_697c83_pratt_timing_test_d28466-Illustration-1-dark.svg" | relative_url }}" alt="Timing Test illustration 1" data-theme-src-dark="{{"/assets/images/alleged_mj12_documen_5d9d42_sparks_greenwood_697c83_pratt_timing_test_d28466-Illustration-1-dark.svg" | relative_url }}" data-theme-src-light="{{"/assets/images/alleged_mj12_documen_5d9d42_sparks_greenwood_697c83_pratt_timing_test_d28466-Illustration-1-light.svg" | relative_url }}" loading="eager" decoding="sync" fetchpriority="high">
This timing argument does not, by itself, prove that the MJ-12 papers were forged. Instead, it changes how historians evaluate them. Rather than asking only whether the documents resemble genuine government records, it asks whether the supposed revelations were genuinely new when they surfaced.

## What Pratt recorded in 1982

According to Sparks and Greenwood, journalist Robert Pratt secretly recorded meetings and telephone conversations with Roswell investigator William L. Moore during 1982 while the two discussed a proposed book based on Moore's contacts with Air Force Office of Special Investigations agent Richard Doty. Pratt preserved tapes, notes and transcripts that remained largely unknown until they were later examined as part of a research project organised through the Center for UFO Studies (CUFOS).<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.academia.edu/24214339/The_Secret_Pratt_Tapes_and_the_Origins_of_MJ_12_Brad_Sparks_and_Barry_Greenwood_" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: academia.edu">[Academia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">academia.edu</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The Secret Pratt Tapes and the Origins of MJ-12 (Brad...&quot;The Secret Pratt Tapes and the Origins of MJ-12&quot; is a paper by US UFO r...</span></span></span>

The significance of the recordings is not merely that they mention UFO secrecy. Sparks and Greenwood argue that they contain discussions of several themes that later became central to the published MJ-12 narrative, including:

* a secret government management structure for recovered UFO material;
* an alleged briefing prepared during the Truman-to-Eisenhower transition in late 1952;
*"[Aquarius]({{'aquarius/' | relative_url }})" briefing material connected with that transition;
* discussions of individuals believed likely to have participated in a highly classified UFO programme.

## What ideas appeared before the film delivery

Sparks and Greenwood identify several examples where they believe the Pratt material anticipates features of the later Eisenhower Briefing Document.

One concerns the importance attached to the late-1952 presidential transition. In the taped conversations, Moore reportedly explains why he regarded the transfer of power from Harry Truman to Dwight Eisenhower as the logical moment for a classified presidential briefing on recovered UFO material. The later Eisenhower Briefing Document is likewise dated November 1952 and centres on precisely such a briefing. Sparks and Greenwood argue that this is evidence that the concept existed in discussion before the document itself appeared.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.academia.edu/24214339/The_Secret_Pratt_Tapes_and_the_Origins_of_MJ_12_Brad_Sparks_and_Barry_Greenwood_" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: academia.edu">[Academia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">academia.edu</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The Secret Pratt Tapes and the Origins of MJ-12 (Brad...&quot;The Secret Pratt Tapes and the Origins of MJ-12&quot; is a paper by US UFO r...</span></span></span>

Another concerns the relationship between the alleged"Aquarius" documents and the later MJ-12 briefing. According to the Pratt transcripts, Moore relayed information from Doty describing an"original Aquarius" briefing associated with the Truman-Eisenhower transition. Sparks and Greenwood argue that this description closely resembles what later emerged publicly as the MJ-12 Eisenhower Briefing Document, suggesting that the two stories developed from the same narrative stream rather than from independent sources.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.academia.edu/24214339/The_Secret_Pratt_Tapes_and_the_Origins_of_MJ_12_Brad_Sparks_and_Barry_Greenwood_" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: academia.edu">[Academia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">academia.edu</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The Secret Pratt Tapes and the Origins of MJ-12 (Brad...&quot;The Secret Pratt Tapes and the Origins of MJ-12&quot; is a paper by US UFO r...</span></span></span>

The researchers also point to Moore's own later acknowledgement that he, Stanton Friedman and Jaime Shandera had already identified nearly all of the eventual alleged MJ-12 [members]({{'members/' | relative_url }}) through archival research before the anonymous film arrived. Sparks and Greenwood interpret this as weakening the argument that the document independently revealed previously unknown information. Instead, they argue that it largely reflected names already regarded within that [research circle]({{'research-circle/' | relative_url }}) as plausible candidates.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.academia.edu/24214339/The_Secret_Pratt_Tapes_and_the_Origins_of_MJ_12_Brad_Sparks_and_Barry_Greenwood_" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: academia.edu">[Academia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">academia.edu</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The Secret Pratt Tapes and the Origins of MJ-12 (Brad...&quot;The Secret Pratt Tapes and the Origins of MJ-12&quot; is a paper by US UFO r...</span></span></span><img src="{{"/assets/images/alleged_mj12_documen_5d9d42_sparks_greenwood_697c83_pratt_timing_test_d28466-Illustration-2-dark.svg" | relative_url }}" alt="Timing Test illustration 2" data-theme-src-dark="{{"/assets/images/alleged_mj12_documen_5d9d42_sparks_greenwood_697c83_pratt_timing_test_d28466-Illustration-2-dark.svg" | relative_url }}" data-theme-src-light="{{"/assets/images/alleged_mj12_documen_5d9d42_sparks_greenwood_697c83_pratt_timing_test_d28466-Illustration-2-light.svg" | relative_url }}" loading="lazy" decoding="async" fetchpriority="low">
## Why prior circulation changes the evidence value

The Pratt tapes matter because they shift the evidential question from [authenticity]({{'authenticity/' | relative_url }}) to [provenance]({{'provenance/' | relative_url }}).

If genuinely classified documents independently disclosed names, dates and organisational concepts unknown outside government, their evidential value would be considerable. However, if the same ideas were already circulating privately among researchers connected with Richard Doty and William Moore, then the later anonymous documents lose much of their force as independent confirmation.

This is the central inference made by Sparks and Greenwood. They argue that apparent agreement between the MJ-12 papers and Moore's earlier research may represent informational feedback rather than corroboration. In other words, the documents may appear convincing precisely because they reproduce concepts already under discussion among the people most likely to receive them.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.academia.edu/24214339/The_Secret_Pratt_Tapes_and_the_Origins_of_MJ_12_Brad_Sparks_and_Barry_Greenwood_" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: academia.edu">[Academia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">academia.edu</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The Secret Pratt Tapes and the Origins of MJ-12 (Brad...&quot;The Secret Pratt Tapes and the Origins of MJ-12&quot; is a paper by US UFO r...</span></span></span>

This reasoning does not require every detail of the documents to have been invented in advance. Instead, it proposes that an existing framework—including the late-1952 briefing, a secret oversight group and candidate committee members—could have provided a template into which later forged or manipulated documents were fitted.<img src="{{"/assets/images/alleged_mj12_documen_5d9d42_sparks_greenwood_697c83_pratt_timing_test_d28466-Illustration-3-dark.svg" | relative_url }}" alt="Timing Test illustration 3" data-theme-src-dark="{{"/assets/images/alleged_mj12_documen_5d9d42_sparks_greenwood_697c83_pratt_timing_test_d28466-Illustration-3-dark.svg" | relative_url }}" data-theme-src-light="{{"/assets/images/alleged_mj12_documen_5d9d42_sparks_greenwood_697c83_pratt_timing_test_d28466-Illustration-3-light.svg" | relative_url }}" loading="lazy" decoding="async" fetchpriority="low">
## Limits of the timing argument

The Pratt tapes are important but not conclusive evidence.

First, much of their significance depends on Sparks and Greenwood's interpretation of the conversations rather than on explicit statements that someone was planning to create future MJ-12 documents. The tapes document discussions and evolving ideas, not an admitted forgery project.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.academia.edu/24214339/The_Secret_Pratt_Tapes_and_the_Origins_of_MJ_12_Brad_Sparks_and_Barry_Greenwood_" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: academia.edu">[Academia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">academia.edu</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The Secret Pratt Tapes and the Origins of MJ-12 (Brad...&quot;The Secret Pratt Tapes and the Origins of MJ-12&quot; is a paper by US UFO r...</span></span></span>

Second, supporters of MJ-12 have argued that prior discussion of secret committees does not necessarily invalidate later leaked documents. Researchers often develop hypotheses before documentary confirmation appears, and coincidence between earlier expectations and later evidence is not impossible.

Sparks and Greenwood respond that the cumulative pattern matters more than any single overlap. Their case relies on multiple correspondences—the timing of the presidential transition, the Aquarius material, committee concepts and anticipated personnel—appearing before the anonymous film delivery.
