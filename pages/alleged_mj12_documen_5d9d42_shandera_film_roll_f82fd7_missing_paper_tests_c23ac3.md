## Introduction

The anonymous 35 mm film sent to Jaime Shandera was enough to reproduce [the alleged MJ-12 documents]({{'the-alleged-mj-12-documents/' | relative_url }}) visually, but it prevented investigators from examining the documents as physical artefacts. That distinction is central to the [authenticity]({{'authenticity/' | relative_url }}) debate. In questioned document examination, the paper itself often carries as much evidential value as the words printed on it. By receiving only photographs, researchers lost the opportunity to test the age of the paper, analyse inks, inspect typewriter impressions, study filing marks, or establish an unbroken chain of custody. Those missing examinations do not prove the documents were forged, but they remove many of the forensic methods that would normally help distinguish an authentic government record from a modern fabrication.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Questioned_document_examination" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Questioned document examination</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Questioned document examination</span></span></span><img src="{{"/assets/images/alleged_mj12_documen_5d9d42_shandera_film_roll_f82fd7_missing_paper_tests_c23ac3-Illustration-1-dark.svg" | relative_url }}" alt="Paper Tests illustration 1" data-theme-src-dark="{{"/assets/images/alleged_mj12_documen_5d9d42_shandera_film_roll_f82fd7_missing_paper_tests_c23ac3-Illustration-1-dark.svg" | relative_url }}" data-theme-src-light="{{"/assets/images/alleged_mj12_documen_5d9d42_shandera_film_roll_f82fd7_missing_paper_tests_c23ac3-Illustration-1-light.svg" | relative_url }}" loading="eager" decoding="sync" fetchpriority="high">
## What original documents can reveal

Authenticating a historical government document usually involves far more than reading its contents. Professional document examiners assess numerous physical characteristics that cannot be recovered from a photographic image.

Key areas include:<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* **Paper composition and manufacture.** Fibre composition, fillers, coatings, watermarks and manufacturing techniques can often indicate when and where paper was produced. A document claiming to originate in 1947 or 1952 should be consistent with paper stocks available during those periods.
* **Ink analysis.** Chemical examination can distinguish different ink formulations, identify later additions and, in some circumstances, estimate whether inks were applied at different times.
* **Typewriter evidence.** Original typed pages preserve microscopic impressions, ribbon characteristics, misaligned characters and pressure marks that can sometimes be linked to a particular machine or typing process.
* **Indented writing.** Pressure from typing or handwriting frequently leaves impressions on underlying sheets. Specialised lighting or electrostatic detection can recover these marks, revealing missing pages or the sequence in which documents were created.
* **Physical wear.** Folding, staple holes, rust stains, paper clips, filing damage and ageing patterns can help establish whether a document experienced ordinary administrative use rather than being produced solely for photography.</div>

## Which tests the film format prevented

The Shandera film effectively froze the alleged MJ-12 papers as images rather than examinable objects. That imposed several practical limitations.

First, investigators could not determine whether the photographed sheets were themselves old, modern, or artificially aged. A photograph records appearance but not the material properties needed for laboratory testing.

Second, no examination of typing impressions was possible. Original typewritten pages retain three-dimensional evidence that disappears when photographed. Subtle embossing, ribbon defects and strike pressure are flattened into a two-dimensional image.

Third, researchers lost access to evidence of document handling. Physical government records often accumulate routine marks such as filing notations, staple damage, hole punches, annotations on reverse sides or edge wear. A carefully framed photograph may omit many of these features entirely.

Fourth, no laboratory could perform destructive or minimally destructive testing. Small samples of paper fibres or ink cannot be analysed if the only surviving evidence is a photographic reproduction.

The result was that debate shifted towards typography, language, formatting, historical consistency and [provenance]({{'provenance/' | relative_url }}) because the underlying physical evidence was unavailable from the outset.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Questioned_document_examination" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Questioned document examination</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Questioned document examination</span></span></span><img src="{{"/assets/images/alleged_mj12_documen_5d9d42_shandera_film_roll_f82fd7_missing_paper_tests_c23ac3-Illustration-2-dark.svg" | relative_url }}" alt="Paper Tests illustration 2" data-theme-src-dark="{{"/assets/images/alleged_mj12_documen_5d9d42_shandera_film_roll_f82fd7_missing_paper_tests_c23ac3-Illustration-2-dark.svg" | relative_url }}" data-theme-src-light="{{"/assets/images/alleged_mj12_documen_5d9d42_shandera_film_roll_f82fd7_missing_paper_tests_c23ac3-Illustration-2-light.svg" | relative_url }}" loading="lazy" decoding="async" fetchpriority="low">
## Why physical custody matters in document claims

The absence of original sheets also meant there was no verifiable chain of custody.

In archival practice, provenance is established by tracing how a document moved from its creator through official filing systems to later custodians. Each transfer creates opportunities for verification. Anonymous photographs bypass nearly all of those checkpoints.

Without original documents, investigators could not answer basic evidential questions such as:<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* Who last possessed the originals?
* Were the photographed pages complete or selectively presented?
* Had the documents been altered before photography?
* Did the originals still exist for independent examination?</div>

## Why later debates relied on indirect evidence

Because no original paper was available, critics and supporters concentrated on evidence that could be extracted from the photographed pages themselves.

Researchers examined issues such as:<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--example" markdown="1">

* formatting conventions used in classified documents;
* date styles and administrative terminology;
* signatures compared with known historical examples;
* whether officials named in the documents were in the claimed locations at the stated times;
* consistency with declassified government records.</div>

Several critics argued that these indirect indicators pointed towards fabrication, while supporters maintained that textual consistency outweighed the lack of originals. The FBI ultimately treated the circulated material as inauthentic, and other government archival reviews likewise reported finding no evidence that the alleged MJ-12 papers were genuine executive-branch records. ## The lasting significance of the missing originals

The Shandera film created an unusual evidential situation. It preserved readable images while permanently limiting what investigators could examine. That distinction continues to shape discussion of the alleged MJ-12 documents.
