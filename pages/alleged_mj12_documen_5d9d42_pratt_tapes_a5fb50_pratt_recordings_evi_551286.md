## Introduction

The Pratt recordings are significant because they preserve conversations about ideas resembling the later MJ-12 narrative before [the alleged MJ-12 documents]({{'the-alleged-mj-12-documents/' | relative_url }}) became public. Rather than providing proof that a secret government committee existed, the recordings are important as a chronological evidence trail. They show that many of the themes later presented as startling revelations—including a presidential briefing, Project Aquarius, an elite oversight group, crashed saucers and named insiders—were already circulating privately among a small network of UFO researchers and alleged intelligence contacts in 1981–82. That makes the tapes relevant to questions of [provenance]({{'provenance/' | relative_url }}): they suggest that core MJ-12 concepts were evolving before the famous documents surfaced, rather than appearing suddenly from an unknown archival source.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.academia.edu/24214339/The_Secret_Pratt_Tapes_and_the_Origins_of_MJ_12_Brad_Sparks_and_Barry_Greenwood_" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: academia.edu">[Academia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">academia.edu</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The Secret Pratt Tapes and the Origins of MJ-12 (Brad...&quot;The Secret Pratt Tapes and the Origins of MJ-12&quot; is a paper by US UFO r...</span></span></span><img src="{{"/assets/images/alleged_mj12_documen_5d9d42_pratt_tapes_a5fb50_pratt_recordings_evi_551286-Illustration-1-dark.svg" | relative_url }}" alt="Pratt Tapes illustration 1" data-theme-src-dark="{{"/assets/images/alleged_mj12_documen_5d9d42_pratt_tapes_a5fb50_pratt_recordings_evi_551286-Illustration-1-dark.svg" | relative_url }}" data-theme-src-light="{{"/assets/images/alleged_mj12_documen_5d9d42_pratt_tapes_a5fb50_pratt_recordings_evi_551286-Illustration-1-light.svg" | relative_url }}" loading="eager" decoding="sync" fetchpriority="high">
## What Robert Pratt reportedly recorded

According to research by Brad Sparks and Barry Greenwood, journalist Robert Pratt secretly recorded meetings and telephone conversations with UFO researcher William L. Moore during 1982. Moore discussed information he said he had received from Air Force Office of Special Investigations ([AFOSI]({{'afosi/' | relative_url }})) Staff Sergeant Richard Doty, who would later become one of the most controversial figures in the history of UFO disinformation claims. The recordings remained largely unknown until Pratt's files were examined years later and presented in a 2007 study.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.academia.edu/24214339/The_Secret_Pratt_Tapes_and_the_Origins_of_MJ_12_Brad_Sparks_and_Barry_Greenwood_" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: academia.edu">[Academia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">academia.edu</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The Secret Pratt Tapes and the Origins of MJ-12 (Brad...&quot;The Secret Pratt Tapes and the Origins of MJ-12&quot; is a paper by US UFO r...</span></span></span>

## Why the 1981–82 timing matters

The value of the Pratt recordings lies primarily in their chronology.

The best-known MJ-12 documents first appeared anonymously in late 1984 and were publicised in 1987. The Pratt recordings pre-date those events by roughly two years. If conversations from 1981–82 already contain many of the later document's central themes, then those themes cannot be treated as entirely novel revelations appearing for the first time in the alleged leaked papers.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.academia.edu/24214339/The_Secret_Pratt_Tapes_and_the_Origins_of_MJ_12_Brad_Sparks_and_Barry_Greenwood_" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: academia.edu">[Academia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">academia.edu</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The Secret Pratt Tapes and the Origins of MJ-12 (Brad...&quot;The Secret Pratt Tapes and the Origins of MJ-12&quot; is a paper by US UFO r...</span></span></span>

Researchers who question the [authenticity]({{'authenticity/' | relative_url }}) of MJ-12 regard this sequence as especially important because it creates a plausible developmental pathway:<img src="{{"/assets/images/alleged_mj12_documen_5d9d42_pratt_tapes_a5fb50_pratt_recordings_evi_551286-Illustration-2-dark.svg" | relative_url }}" alt="Pratt Tapes illustration 2" data-theme-src-dark="{{"/assets/images/alleged_mj12_documen_5d9d42_pratt_tapes_a5fb50_pratt_recordings_evi_551286-Illustration-2-dark.svg" | relative_url }}" data-theme-src-light="{{"/assets/images/alleged_mj12_documen_5d9d42_pratt_tapes_a5fb50_pratt_recordings_evi_551286-Illustration-2-light.svg" | relative_url }}" loading="lazy" decoding="async" fetchpriority="low"><div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--step-flow" markdown="1">

## What the recordings do and do not prove

The Pratt recordings are frequently misunderstood because they are sometimes presented either as decisive proof of MJ-12 or as proof of deliberate fraud. The evidence supports neither conclusion on its own.

What the recordings do support is the existence of an early circulation network for MJ-12-related concepts. They demonstrate that ideas later embedded in the alleged documents were already being exchanged among Moore, Pratt and alleged intelligence sources before the public appearance of the MJ-12 papers. This weakens claims that the documents introduced entirely unknown information.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.academia.edu/24214339/The_Secret_Pratt_Tapes_and_the_Origins_of_MJ_12_Brad_Sparks_and_Barry_Greenwood_" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: academia.edu">[Academia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">academia.edu</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The Secret Pratt Tapes and the Origins of MJ-12 (Brad...&quot;The Secret Pratt Tapes and the Origins of MJ-12&quot; is a paper by US UFO r...</span></span></span>

They do **not** demonstrate that:<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* an official MJ-12 committee actually existed;
* Richard Doty's claims were accurate;<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=osp3fFdlc8I" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: youtube.com">[youtube.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">youtube.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">The Myth of Majestic 12: UFO Disinformation or the Ultimate Cover-Up?</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">MJ-12 documents forgery Richard Doty William Moore TIME TRAVELING UFOS - “They Were Us from the Future” &#124; Ancient Aliens &#124; #Shorts &#124; Hist...</span></span></span>
* the later documents were necessarily forged by any specific individual;
* every detail discussed on the tapes was fabricated.</div>

Instead, they document conversations, not verified historical events. ## Why the Pratt tapes remain central to MJ-12 provenance debates

For researchers studying the alleged MJ-12 documents, the Pratt recordings are valuable less for what they say about extraterrestrials than for what they reveal about document origins.
